# SUBJECT: BU-209 ASSIGNMENT

## PROBLEM: CRASHING OF BABCOCK UMIS

### Who has the problem?

Every student and staff member in Babcock has faced one or two issues concerning the UMIS portal. The most common of these problems is crashing due to high activity.

### How do they cope?

The way everyone copes is different, but here are some examples:

1. Due to high traffic, students open the UMIS during odd times of the day (1am–3am).
2. Some people wait until it opens, which can take up to 3 days or more.

### Survey

After many surveys, we found out that the issue has been discussed a lot, but there seemed to be no change in the UMIS page. Many students were in favour of turning the UMIS portal into an app, which would be easier to access and maintain.

### Solution: Babcock UMIS App

Turning the UMIS into an app is a very big upgrade to the current UMIS website. It has been proven that apps are faster to launch, even in bad network circumstances.

| Feature | Apps | Website |
|---|---|---|
| Better loading speed | Effective | Less effective |
| Data caching | Effective | Less effective |

### The MVP

1. **Performance and speed upgrade**
   - Faster application launch
   - Background data synchronization
   - Optimized data usage

2. **Easier navigation**

   The services will be organized into clear selections, making them easier to use.

3. **Biometric login**

   Allow devices to make use of fingerprint and facial recognition for better security.

4. **Provides university announcements**
   - Calendar change
   - Public holidays
   - Registration closure
